# Retired early pilots

The 100-update recurrence grid and 1,000-update continuation established that the pipeline trained and that additional refinement became useful with more fitting. They used the old layout and small corpus; they are not the serious baseline.

The `initial/REPORT.md` and `continuation/REPORT.md` reports preserve the conclusions. Small JSON/CSV/log/plot artifacts remain in their local ignored `results/` directories. Their embedded paths are historical. Obsolete launch/config/plot scripts and large checkpoints were deleted in the September 2026 cleanup. The old smoke checkpoints were also deleted; smoke configs and validation notes remain. These experiments can no longer be resumed from this checkout.

The architecture-site ablation, cross-backend supervision pilot, and baseline LR selection retain their checkpoints and source definitions outside this archive.
